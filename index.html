        Early Access Terms & Privacy
        Early access

        Forge is a controlled beta MVP.

        Forge helps collect local job, worker, referral, bid, and follow-up information so the operator can manually match work with local people. The MVP is not a payment processor, escrow service, background-check provider, insurance provider, or emergency service.

        What Forge collects

        Names, phone numbers, emails, job details, worker profile details, bids, referrals, notes, and messages entered into the MVP.

        How it is used

        Forge uses this information to follow up by text, phone, or email, coordinate early job matching, and improve the first local launch group.

        Payments

        No payment is collected in this MVP. Keep deposits, payment, disputes, and contracts outside Forge until the production trust workflow is ready.

        Safety

        Users should verify identity, licensing, insurance, pricing, job scope, and worksite safety before work begins.

        Privacy basics

        Controlled beta privacy commitments

        • Forge stores MVP data locally in this browser unless a webhook or backend is connected.
        • Admins should export backups before demos, resets, imports, or public testing.
        • Only collect contact info from people who agreed to early access follow-up.
        • Do not enter passwords, payment cards, bank information, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive documents into this MVP.
        • Before broad public launch, Forge needs final legal review, real authentication, backend storage, backup policy, and trust and safety workflow.
