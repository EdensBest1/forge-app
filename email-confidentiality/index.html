Email Confidentiality, Proprietary Information & Limited-Use Policy

Effective: August 17, 2026 Version: 1.0 Contact: Admin@HireOnForge.com
This policy records Forge's confidentiality expectations and information-handling instructions. It does not replace a signed nondisclosure agreement, non-circumvention agreement, intellectual-property assignment, master services agreement, referral agreement, or transaction document.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies when Forge, Eden's Best, or an identified affiliate sends business information by email or attachment. It is intended to identify potentially confidential or proprietary information, limit disclosure to the stated business purpose, preserve a written record of handling expectations, and direct the parties to use signed agreements for binding deal terms.

2. Covered information

Covered information may include business and product concepts, marketplace and software concepts, operating models, workflows, automation and agent architectures, source code, prompts, specifications, wireframes, prototypes, designs, plans, strategies, pricing, margins, forecasts, customer or supplier information, worker and provider information, lead lists, opportunity sources, introductions, proposals, bids, scopes, deal structures, negotiating positions, licensing strategies, compliance procedures, trade secrets, know-how, and attachments.

3. Limited authorized use

Covered information is provided only for the purpose identified in the applicable communication, proposal, meeting, pilot, evaluation, or signed agreement. Without prior written authorization, a recipient should not copy, publish, distribute, sell, license, disclose, use to compete, use to bypass Forge, or use to contact introduced parties outside the authorized process.

4. Ownership and no implied license

Disclosure does not transfer ownership or grant an implied license, partnership, agency, exclusivity, financing commitment, employment relationship, authority to bind another party, or permission to use any brand, software, design, data, image, content, invention, or other intellectual property. Any such rights or commitments must be stated in an executed written agreement signed by authorized representatives.

5. Safeguards and misdelivery

Recipients should limit access to people with a legitimate need to know and an applicable confidentiality duty, use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, avoid forwarding protected information to unapproved systems, report suspected loss or misuse, and return or delete information when lawfully requested. A person receiving a message in error should notify the sender and delete the message and attachments.

6. Required and protected disclosures

Nothing in this policy is intended to prohibit disclosure required by law, subpoena, court order, or valid governmental process; confidential communication with legal counsel; reporting or investigating a suspected legal violation; or any whistleblower, labor, regulatory, or other disclosure protected by non-waivable law.

7. Binding agreements control

The email footer and this policy are evidence of confidentiality expectations and handling instructions. When an executed agreement applies, that agreement controls to the extent of any conflict. Material business concepts, introductions, source relationships, technical information, regulated activity, and high-value transactions should be disclosed only after the appropriate agreement and internal approval gates are in place.

8. Protection stack

Forge may also use trademark clearance and registration, copyright registration for eligible expression, patent review before public disclosure, signed NDAs and non-circumvention agreements, IP assignments, work-made-for-hire provisions, access controls, encryption, role-based permissions, data classification, logging, secure storage, controlled data rooms, and documented development history.
