# College Weekly Campaign Plan

## Goal

Create an education-first College Weekly launch lane for Admitly inside Forge without mixing student admissions marketing with Stitch/cannabis.

## First 7 steps

1. Confirm current College Weekly contact path.
2. Send one short email with no fake promises.
3. Share the one-page concept only after interest.
4. Build campaign landing page at /admitly/college-weekly.
5. Track replies manually.
6. Keep high school and admissions audiences away from cannabis content.
7. Review copy before any automated sending.

## Metrics

- Replies
- Booked calls
- Landing page visits
- Student/parent/counselor interest
- Campus ambassador interest
- Opt-outs
