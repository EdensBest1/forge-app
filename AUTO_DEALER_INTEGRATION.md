# Forge Auto Dealer Integration

Current release candidate: `v70`

Forge Auto Services can show dealer partners and collect auto service requests in the MVP, but public lead routing should wait until each auto partner has approved contact, intake, qualification, and compliance details.

## Dealer Partners

- S&A Auto: featured Forge Auto sales partnership.
- JoCo Auto Sales: Medford dealer partner path.
- Chevelles Auto Sales: dealer partner lead.
- Shasta Lake Auto Sales: Shasta Lake dealer partner lead.

## Setup Required Per Auto Partner

- Approved contact person.
- Lead intake phone and email.
- Website or inventory source.
- Dealer, mechanic, detailing, transport, or service terms for customer handoff.
- Expected response time for Forge leads.
- Compliance review for title, financing, disclosures, payment, repair authorization, towing, transport, insurance-related work, and test-drive safety.

## MVP Boundary

Forge does not verify title, ownership, financing, mechanical condition, dealer licensing, repair licensing, towing authority, insurance coverage, transport authority, or payment in this MVP. Vehicle sales, financing, repair, towing, transport, and insurance-related work must be performed by properly licensed or qualified partners where required.

## Next Build Step

After approved partner details are available, add real contact fields, inventory source links, service routing rules, and a backend lead-delivery log for each Forge Auto request or buyer inquiry.
