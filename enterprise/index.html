30-90 day pilot

Start narrow. Prove performance. Expand carefully.

A pilot can focus on one geography, one service category, or one recurring workforce problem. The final structure depends on employer requirements, applicable law, worker classification, procurement, insurance, and available verified capacity.

Week 1: Discovery and requirements
Stakeholders, workforce scope, procurement, legal/compliance review, KPIs, and approval path.
Weeks 2-3: Supply verification
Worker/provider recruitment, screening, trade requirements, geography, availability, and backup capacity.
Weeks 3-6: Controlled fulfillment
Limited dispatch or service pilot with human approvals, operational tracking, incident handling, and closeout evidence.
Weeks 6-12: Expansion decision
Review fill rate, response time, quality, compliance, cost, and whether a larger rollout is justified.
