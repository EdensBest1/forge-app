## Money-Control Requirements

- No single person may create a vendor, change a payout account, approve an invoice, and release a payout.
- Bank account changes require callback verification and second approval.
- Every payout must connect to signed contract, invoice, approval, completion/deal record, settlement statement, DocuSign envelope ID, Google Drive archive link, and Monday.com item.
- Stitch and Forge must not hold customer funds, claim escrow status, or act as a bank, money transmitter, or payment processor unless counsel and an approved payment partner authorize the flow.
