# Email Confidentiality, Proprietary Information, Limited-Use, and Non-Circumvention Policy

**Owner:** Forge / applicable Eden's Best affiliate identified in the message  
**Operational contact:** Admin@HireOnForge.com  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** August 17, 2026  
**Status:** Operational policy and notice; attorney review recommended before reliance in a material transaction.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the confidentiality expectations, permitted-use limits, ownership position, and handling instructions that apply when Forge or an applicable Eden's Best affiliate sends business information by email or attachment.

The standardized email footer is intended to:

1. identify communications that may contain confidential, proprietary, trade-secret, commercially sensitive, or legally protected information;
2. communicate that the information is disclosed for a limited business purpose;
3. create a consistent written record of the sender's confidentiality expectations and information-handling instructions; and
4. direct recipients to use signed transaction documents for binding confidentiality, non-circumvention, intellectual-property ownership, exclusivity, referral, fee, and deal terms.

## 2. Covered information

Covered information may include, without limitation:

- business concepts, product concepts, marketplace concepts, software concepts, operating models, workflows, automation designs, agent architectures, source code, prompts, specifications, wireframes, prototypes, designs, plans, roadmaps, strategies, pricing, margins, budgets, forecasts, financial information, customer or supplier information, worker or provider information, lead lists, opportunity sources, introductions, deal structures, negotiating positions, proposals, bids, scopes, contracts, licensing strategies, regulatory information, compliance procedures, trade secrets, know-how, and attachments;
- information marked confidential, proprietary, restricted, internal, trade secret, or similar; and
- information that a reasonable recipient should understand is confidential from its nature, context, commercial sensitivity, or the circumstances of disclosure.

## 3. Authorized purpose and limited use

Covered information is provided only for the purpose expressly identified in the email, attachment, proposal, meeting, pilot, evaluation, or written agreement. Unless the sender gives prior written authorization, a recipient must not:

- use the information for any unrelated purpose;
- copy, reproduce, publish, distribute, disclose, sell, license, transmit, scrape, train a model on, or otherwise exploit the information;
- use the information to compete with, bypass, disintermediate, or circumvent Forge or the identified affiliate;
- contact or transact directly with a protected lead, worker, provider, customer, supplier, investor, lender, contractor, licensee, or introduced party outside the authorized process;
- reverse engineer, decompile, derive, replicate, or create a competing implementation from protected technical materials, except to the extent a signed agreement or non-waivable law expressly permits it; or
- remove confidentiality, attribution, copyright, trademark, proprietary, watermark, source, or access-control notices.

## 4. Ownership and no implied license

All rights, title, and interest in covered information remain with the lawful owner. Disclosure does not grant any license, assignment, ownership interest, joint venture, partnership, agency, franchise, distributorship, exclusivity, right of first refusal, financing commitment, employment relationship, authority to bind another party, or permission to use any trademark, service mark, copyright, patent right, trade secret, data, software, image, likeness, or other intellectual property.

Any license, transfer, ownership allocation, work-made-for-hire term, assignment, revenue share, referral fee, commission, exclusivity, non-circumvention obligation, or transaction commitment must be stated in an executed written agreement signed by authorized representatives.

## 5. Recipient safeguards

A recipient handling covered information should:

- limit access to personnel and professional advisers with a legitimate need to know and an applicable duty of confidentiality;
- use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards;
- avoid forwarding protected information to personal accounts or unapproved systems;
- notify the sender promptly of suspected loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or misdelivery;
- preserve relevant evidence when an incident or dispute is reasonably anticipated; and
- return or securely delete covered information when requested, subject to legal holds, mandatory retention rules, and ordinary backup limitations.

## 6. Misdelivery

A person who receives a message in error should notify the sender, refrain from reviewing or using the information beyond what is necessary to identify the error, and delete the message and attachments from active systems, subject to applicable law.

## 7. Required and protected disclosures

Nothing in this policy is intended to prohibit or restrict:

- a disclosure required by law, subpoena, court order, or valid governmental process, provided the recipient gives legally permitted notice and reasonable assistance to seek confidential treatment;
- confidential communication with legal counsel;
- reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or
- any whistleblower, labor, regulatory, or other disclosure protected by non-waivable law.

## 8. Relationship to signed agreements

The email footer and this policy are evidence of the sender's confidentiality expectations and handling instructions. They do not replace a signed nondisclosure agreement, non-circumvention agreement, intellectual-property assignment, master services agreement, data-processing agreement, referral agreement, pilot agreement, licensing agreement, term sheet, purchase agreement, employment or contractor agreement, or other transaction document.

When an executed agreement applies, that agreement controls to the extent of any conflict. Material business concepts, source relationships, introductions, deal structures, technical information, and regulated or high-value transactions should not be disclosed until the appropriate written agreement and internal approval gates are in place.

## 9. Brand and intellectual-property protection

The email notice is only one layer of protection. The owner should also use, as applicable:

- trademark clearance and registration for brand names, logos, and source identifiers;
- copyright registration for eligible original text, software, graphics, photographs, audiovisual works, and other protectable expression;
- patent evaluation before public disclosure of potentially patentable inventions;
- signed NDAs, non-circumvention agreements, IP assignments, work-made-for-hire provisions, invention-assignment agreements, and transaction documents;
- access controls, encryption, role-based permissions, data classification, logging, secure storage, retention rules, and incident response;
- confidential legends on sensitive documents and controlled data rooms; and
- documented ownership, authorship, development history, source files, dates, versions, disclosures, and approvals.

## 10. Standard signature notice

Use the following notice beneath the approved business signature:

> **CONFIDENTIALITY, PROPRIETARY INFORMATION & LIMITED-USE NOTICE:** This communication and any attachments may contain confidential, proprietary, trade-secret, privileged, or commercially sensitive information belonging to Forge, Eden's Best, and/or an identified affiliate. It is provided solely for evaluating or carrying out the identified business relationship and may not be copied, disclosed, distributed, used to compete, used to bypass Forge, or used to contact introduced parties outside the authorized process without prior written permission. Receipt does not grant any license, ownership interest, partnership, agency, exclusivity, financing commitment, or authority to bind any party. If you are not the intended recipient, notify the sender and delete all copies. This notice records the sender's confidentiality expectations and handling instructions; binding confidentiality, non-circumvention, IP ownership, exclusivity, fees, and transaction terms require an executed written agreement. Nothing here restricts legally protected disclosures or disclosures required by law.

## 11. Administration

The approved signature and notice should be used consistently on external business-development, partnership, proposal, investor, licensing, procurement, workforce, marketplace, financing-referral, and transaction emails sent from approved company accounts.

No employee, contractor, agent, or system may alter the notice to claim that:

- the footer alone creates an NDA or signed contract;
- the recipient has accepted financial, employment, procurement, licensing, or transaction terms merely by receiving the email;
- Forge is a bank, lender, law firm, licensed contractor, employer of record, staffing agency, or regulated cannabis operator unless the exact statement is true and approved for the relevant entity and jurisdiction; or
- a deal, worker, provider, license, financing product, project, approval, rate, capacity, or outcome is guaranteed.

Material deviations require legal and executive approval.
