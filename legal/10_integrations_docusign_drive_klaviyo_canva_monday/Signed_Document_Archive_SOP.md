Generated: 2026-06-27

**Template only - attorney review required before external use.**

## Workflow

1. User completes onboarding/intake.
2. App determines correct document package.
3. DocuSign envelope is created.
4. Signer signs.
5. Completed PDF and certificate/audit record save to Google Drive.
6. Monday.com item updates with document status, envelope ID, signed PDF link, signer email, signed timestamp, and next action.
7. Klaviyo profile updates with segment/status.
8. App dashboard shows signed/unsigned status.
9. Admin sees missing paperwork before any work, deal, payment, route, reveal, or payout moves forward.
