# Forge Early Launch Workflow

Use this while collecting the first real jobs, workers, and referrals.

## Tonight / In-person

1. Open Forge to `Quick Capture`.
2. If someone shows interest, save their name, phone, lead type, priority, and note.
3. Mark hot leads as `Hot`.
4. Use `Copy Invite Text` if they want the link.

## Daily Admin Pass

1. Open `Admin`.
2. Copy the daily brief.
3. Contact every `Hot` and `New` lead.
4. Mark people `Contacted` after texting or calling.
5. Export a JSON backup at the end of the day.

## First Traction Targets

- 10 job leads
- 20 worker leads
- 25 referrals
- 3 actual bid conversations
- 1 completed paid job

## Follow-up Script

```text
Hey, this is Forge. Good meeting you. I saved your info because you mentioned local work / needing workers. Can I send you the early access link and follow up tomorrow?
```

## What To Learn

- Do people understand what Forge does in one sentence?
- Are job posters willing to leave contact info?
- Are workers willing to join before jobs are guaranteed?
- Which category gets interest fastest?
- What trust concern comes up most often?
