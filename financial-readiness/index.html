Controlled service plan

Four steps toward financial readiness

The planned service is intentionally narrow: organize what already exists, surface questions, and prepare reviewed work for an authorized person or licensed professional.

01

Record organization

Build a consistent record map for statements, receipts, invoices, notices, and prior-period files.

Draft labels and checklists only; no public document intake is active.

02

Reconciliation support

Compare approved source records, flag differences, and prepare an exception list for human review.

The customer or bookkeeper confirms every correction.

03

Reporting readiness

Prepare draft monthly-close checklists, category questions, and management-summary outlines.

No autonomous journal entries, payments, or account changes.

04

Professional handoff

Assemble a reviewed question list and records index for a CPA, enrolled agent, or qualified preparer.

Forge does not prepare, sign, submit, or represent a customer on a tax return.

Human-controlled by design

The prototype prepares low-risk internal work. Production remains gated.

During controlled development, the non-networked policy prototype has no external effects. Nothing should be sent, filed, signed, paid, disputed, or changed without the authorized customer's review and explicit approval.
