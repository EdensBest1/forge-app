Medford FAQ

Know the boundaries before work begins.

Does Forge guarantee a contractor?

No. Forge accepts and reviews requests; compatible coverage must still be confirmed.

Are contractors licensed and insured?

Not automatically. Information may be self-reported until reviewed. Confirm requirements, evidence, scope, and contracts before work.

How far outside Medford can I post?

Use the actual city, ZIP, and county. Contractors provide their own service radius and travel area.

Does Forge collect payment?

No payment, escrow, deposit, payout, or dispatch is active in this controlled-beta experience.
