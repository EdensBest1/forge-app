New York FAQ

Keep building and contractor requirements explicit.

Can I post for any borough?

Yes. Use the actual city and ZIP. Contractor coverage still depends on service area, access, category, and availability.

Does Forge approve building work?

No. Building management, permits, certificates, trade requirements, contracts, and safety remain outside Forge approval.

Are contractor credentials checked?

They may be reviewed later, but initial license and insurance information is explicitly self-reported.

Does a quote start work?

No. Quote review, revision, cancellation, and dispute states do not create payment, dispatch, or a binding project contract.
