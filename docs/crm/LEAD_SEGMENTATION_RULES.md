# Lead Segmentation Rules

Segmentation is for prioritization only. It is not a legal classification and must be reviewed before outreach or writeback.

## Stitch Segments

- Farm / Producer: producer, farm, grow, cultivation, harvest, biomass, flower, trim.
- Processor: processor, manufacturer, extraction, edible, gummy, concentrate, pre-roll.
- Lab: lab, testing, analytical, compliance testing.
- Dispensary / Retailer: dispensary, retailer, retail store.
- Wholesaler: wholesaler, distributor, bulk buying, brokered purchasing.
- Logistics / Transport: transport, logistics, delivery, route, courier.
- Broker / Admin Review: broker, unclear intermediary, compliance ambiguity.
- Unknown / Needs Review: any lead that does not confidently match.

## Forge Segments

- Farm jobs / labor / harvest / cleanup: producer, farm, cultivation, field labor, harvest, trimming, cleanup.
- Facility maintenance: processor, retailer, lab, warehouse, maintenance, repair.
- Construction / trades: buildout, contractor, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, framing, remodel.
- Cleaning / Airbnb / property help: cleaning, turnovers, rental, home/business property help.
- Auto / transport: fleet, driver, delivery, transport, logistics.
- Business growth / marketing: weak website, no Google presence, stale social, need leads.
- Manufacturing / packaging / nutraceutical crossover: processor, packaging, bottling, nutraceutical, production line.
- Home services: owner or operator also has home/property needs.
- Unknown / Needs Review: insufficient signal.

## North Star Segments

- No/weak website
- Weak Google Business profile
- Needs social media
- Needs photography/video
- Needs SEO
- Needs ads
- Needs CRM/email/SMS
- Has multiple businesses
- High-value brand opportunity
- Unknown / Needs Review

## Manual Review Fields

- License status
- Contact consent
- Do-not-contact
- Duplicate risk
- Recent contact history
- Notes that change fit
