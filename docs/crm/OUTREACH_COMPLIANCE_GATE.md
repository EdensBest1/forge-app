# Outreach Compliance Gate

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Attorney/compliance review is required before scaled outreach.

## Hard Stops

- Do not auto-text.
- Do not auto-email.
- Do not contact do-not-contact or suppressed records.
- Do not use ATDS/prerecorded/artificial voice or automated SMS for marketing without appropriate consent review.
- Do not make guaranteed sales, guaranteed buyer, guaranteed job, or legal approval claims.
- Do not give regulated cannabis legal/compliance advice.
- Do not send links containing phone, email, owner name, address, or license number.

## Before Any Outreach Wave

- Deduped list reviewed.
- Consent fields reviewed.
- Suppression list applied.
- Wave 1 capped at top 50 per category.
- Message is role-specific and short.
- Every contact attempt is logged.
- Opt-out requests are honored.
- Manual approval is recorded.
