# Photography & Videography Overview

## Status

Photography & Videography is implemented and live in the Forge UI. Customers can request local creative work, and photographers or videographers can apply as approved providers.

## Services Offered

- Wedding photography and videography
- Engagement and couples shoots
- Event photography and videography
- Business branding shoots
- Social media reels and short-form video
- Real estate and property photography
- Product and brand photography
- Family portraits
- Church and community events
- Music videos and creative content
- Editing-only work
- Drone photo/video add-ons when qualified and approved

## Customer Flow

1. Customer opens the Photography & Videography page.
2. Customer submits the creative request form with contact info, service type, date, location, media type, budget, delivery timeline, shot list, and optional inspiration files.
3. Forge saves the request with category value `photography_videography`.
4. Admin reviews the request, confirms details, and matches it only with approved local creative providers.
5. Final provider terms, pricing, deliverables, cancellation rules, usage rights, and delivery timeline should be confirmed before booking.

## Provider Flow

1. Photographer or videographer opens the provider application form.
2. Provider submits contact info, service area, portfolio, experience, gear, editing tools, availability, rates, drone capability, insurance notes, and profile materials.
3. Forge saves the provider lead with category/provider value `photography_videography`.
4. Admin reviews portfolio, availability, provider terms, insurance, drone qualification if relevant, and service fit before matching.
5. Approved providers may be matched with customer creative requests.

## Privacy And Safety Notes

- Customer contact information is for Forge booking and provider matching only.
- Do not collect passwords, payment cards, IDs, bank details, SSNs, or sensitive official documents through this MVP.
- Uploaded files currently need secure storage before broad launch.
- Providers should not be publicly featured until admin review is complete.
- Drone services require qualification, insurance, location/weather review, and any required legal approvals.
- Family/minor shoots need specific consent and release handling before public or portfolio use.
- Event and wedding shoots need clear permission and delivery expectations.

## Launch Risks

- Secure upload storage is not production-ready.
- Media release, usage rights, copyright, portfolio rights, and client delivery terms need attorney review.
- Provider vetting workflow needs documented approval criteria.
- Drone/insurance handling needs stronger review before public promotion.
- Privacy policy and terms need updates for photo/video uploads, provider matching, and creative deliverables.
