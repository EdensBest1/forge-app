# Forge Academy

Forge Academy is the Forge-facing blue-collar career pathway surface. It helps local workers and future workers start career intake for trade school, apprenticeships, union pathways, certifications, resumes, and local job placement support powered by Admitly.

## MVP Surface

- Public route: `/forge-academy`
- Apply route: `/forge-academy/apply`
- Employer route: `/forge-academy/employers`
- School route: `/forge-academy/schools`
- Career dashboard route: `/dashboard/career`
- Admin route: `/admin/forge-academy`
- App screen: `#forge-academy`

## Captured Lead Fields

- Full name, phone, optional email
- City and state
- Desired trade
- Current experience
- Transportation and driver's license status
- Needs training, needs job now, needs resume, and interested in Forge Career+
- Consent to contact
- Notes, status, priority

## Partner Intake

Forge Academy also captures employer/training partners and school/program partners:

- Employers: business, contact, trade category, hiring needs, apprenticeship availability, willingness to train, and license/insurance notes.
- Schools/programs: program name, contact, program types, location, cost range, financial aid availability, deadlines, and notes.

## Safety Rules

- Forge is not a school, licensed recruiter, union, financial aid office, lender, or employer of record.
- Verify employer license, insurance, safety expectations, pay, and written partner terms before routing workers.
- Verify school accreditation, costs, deadlines, outcomes, financial aid, and licensing fit before recommending a program.
- Do not collect sensitive documents or credentials in the browser MVP.
