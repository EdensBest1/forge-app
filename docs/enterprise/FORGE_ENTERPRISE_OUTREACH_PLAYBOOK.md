# Forge Enterprise Outreach Playbook

## Operating rule

Every message must be personalized to a verified public business contact or submitted through an official business contact/procurement route. Complete suppression and opt-out checks. Bulk external messaging, partnership claims, pricing, worker-availability claims, and commitments require human approval.

## H. Discovery questions

1. Which project, region, property, shift, or service category has the most recurring capacity pressure?
2. Are you seeking employees, temporary labor through a licensed staffing model, independent contractors, licensed specialty contractors, service companies, or a mix?
3. What vendor registration, insurance, bonding, safety, union, prevailing-wage, background, drug-testing, certification, or site-access requirements apply?
4. What skills, licenses, equipment, language, schedule, and supervision are required?
5. How many people or crews are needed, and when?
6. What is the minimum acceptable fill rate, response time, and quality standard?
7. Who approves the scope, pricing, provider, contract, and dispatch?
8. What system should receive status and reporting?
9. Would a Forge app demonstration be useful before scoping a pilot?
10. What would make a 30-90 day pilot successful enough to expand?

## I. Objection handling

**“Are you a staffing agency?”**  
Forge is a controlled marketplace and operating platform. The final model depends on the engagement. Forge will not represent itself as a licensed staffing agency, employer of record, or contractor unless the applicable structure, licenses, insurance, and agreements are in place. A pilot begins with requirements and legal/commercial review.

**“How many workers do you have?”**  
Forge maintains a separate capacity inventory. We do not quote a workforce number until the relevant trade, geography, availability, classification, licenses, insurance, and readiness are verified.

**“Are the workers vetted?”**  
Qualification is requirement-specific. Forge tracks the evidence required for the pilot and does not claim a person or company is verified until the applicable evidence is reviewed.

**“Can you guarantee coverage?”**  
No guarantee is made before a written scope, verified capacity, backup plan, and approved terms.

**“Do you work with Flex?”**  
Forge has a Flex referral and partnership strategy, but public partnership language, links, integrations, and commercial terms require Flex’s approval and signed terms. Flex handles its own applications, approvals, onboarding, and products.

## J. Pre-send checklist

- The business and current role are verified from an official/public source.
- The email or contact route is a public business channel.
- No private personal data was inferred or purchased without lawful authority.
- The account is not suppressed, opted out, or do-not-contact.
- The message does not claim a current opening without evidence.
- The message does not promise workers, coverage, price, licensing, insurance, or outcomes.
- Partnership and customer claims are approved.
- Personalization is accurate.
- The correct sender identity and physical/business contact information are present.
- The send is individually approved or falls within an approved campaign limit.
- The CRM record and next action are ready.
