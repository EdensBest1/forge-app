# Forge Enterprise Workforce + App Launch

**Launch date:** August 17, 2026  
**Operating system:** Nexus 60-agent roster  
**Primary offer:** A controlled, human-reviewed enterprise pilot that combines qualified blue-collar workforce/provider sourcing with the Forge intake, matching, workflow, and reporting layer.

## 1. Strategic correction and Flex opportunity

The current Flex opportunity should be described accurately:

- Flex announced a **$70 million Series B1 on July 14, 2026**.
- Flex reported **$180 million in total equity and $300 million in total debt** after the round.
- Reuters reported a valuation of approximately **$1.2 billion**; $1.2 billion is not the amount Flex raised.
- Flex says construction and wholesale are two of its three largest customer categories by logo count.
- Flex's public partnership program is built for operators with networks of business owners, and it currently offers referral partnerships while selectively opening Flex as a Service.

That makes the best Forge-to-Flex proposition:

1. **Distribution:** Forge introduces qualified construction, wholesale, property-service, logistics, and other middle-market business owners through an approved Flex partner path.
2. **Platform integration:** Explore Flex as a Service, co-branded/programmatic accounts, or approved finance workflows inside Forge after a signed agreement and technical/compliance review.
3. **Operational support:** Offer a managed workforce/service pilot where Flex, its customers, or approved partners have real field-operational demand.
4. **App demonstration:** Show how Forge and Nexus capture demand, build verified supply, route work, preserve approvals, and report outcomes.

## 2. Campaign outcome

The first operating target is not mass email volume. It is a reliable pipeline:

`verified account -> verified public business contact -> suppression check -> personalized draft -> human approval -> send -> reply -> discovery -> scope -> pilot -> report -> expand`

The campaign begins with:

- 60 seed enterprise accounts.
- One initial research account assigned to each Nexus agent.
- One governed enterprise database.
- One workforce-capacity inventory.
- One segmented outreach playbook.
- One public enterprise intake page.
- One Flex strategic partnership pitch.
- One daily internal campaign workflow with no autonomous external sends.

## 3. Public positioning

### Core statement

Forge Enterprise helps contractors, property operators, facilities teams, logistics companies, builders, and other employers request qualified workers, crews, contractors, and service providers through a controlled, human-reviewed workflow.

### What Forge may offer

- Employer demand intake.
- Public-account and procurement research.
- Worker, crew, contractor, and service-business recruiting.
- Trade, geography, availability, license, insurance, and qualification tracking.
- Managed pilot design.
- Scheduling and dispatch planning.
- Proof-of-work and quality-control workflow.
- Forge app demonstration and pilot workspace.
- Nexus-assisted CRM, proposals, follow-up, analytics, security, and reporting.

### Claims Forge must not make without evidence

- A company has a current opening merely because its sector commonly uses blue-collar labor.
- A specific worker or crew is available before availability is verified.
- A worker, contractor, or service company is licensed, insured, background-checked, or approved before evidence is reviewed.
- Forge is an employer of record, staffing agency, licensed contractor, Flex representative, lender, bank, or approved vendor unless a signed agreement and applicable licenses support that language.
- Pricing, fill rate, response time, geographic coverage, or workforce quantity that has not been documented.

## 5. Database standard

Every account record must include:

- Account ID.
- Legal/company name.
- Segment and geography.
- Potential workforce/service demand, clearly marked as a hypothesis.
- Forge pitch angle.
- Official source URL.
- Public business email and phone, only when verified.
- Decision-maker role and public professional contact source.
- Contact-verification status and last verification date.
- Priority, stage, next action, and due date.
- Research owner, sales owner, CRM owner.
- Outreach approval, suppression, opt-out, and activity history.
- Procurement/vendor route.
- App-demo interest and managed-pilot interest.
- Notes and evidence links.

## 6. Workforce-capacity standard

A worker, crew, contractor, or service business is not "enterprise ready" until the applicable fields are reviewed:

- Identity/business verification.
- Trade and skills.
- Service area.
- Availability and worker/crew count.
- Classification/employer relationship.
- License requirement and verification.
- Insurance requirement and verification.
- Safety and certification requirements.
- Background-check status through an authorized process when legally relevant.
- Rate assumptions and commercial model.
- References/performance evidence.
- Human approval.

## 7. 30-day launch

### Days 1-3

- Deploy database, 60-agent assignment matrix, enterprise intake page, API route, and reporting workflow.
- Correct Flex financing language everywhere.
- Verify Gmail/CRM/webhook connections.
- Prepare Flex partnership email and contact-form submission.
- Begin public-contact enrichment for Priority 1 accounts.

### Days 4-7

- Verify at least 25 Priority 1 accounts and 10 decision-maker roles.
- Recruit and review the first provider/worker capacity records by trade and geography.
- Run five human-approved outreach messages, not a bulk blast.
- Book the first discovery calls.
- Finalize a standard employer discovery brief and managed-pilot scope.

### Days 8-14

- Reach 50 verified public account/contact routes.
- Conduct at least five discovery conversations.
- Produce three tailored pilot proposals.
- Publish the enterprise landing page and approved credibility content.
- Create worker/provider recruiting campaigns for actual demand gaps.

### Days 15-30

- Secure one controlled pilot or signed channel-partner next step.
- Build verified backup capacity for the pilot.
- Track fill rate, response time, qualifications, safety issues, quality, and client feedback.
- Produce a case study only after written permission and evidence review.
- Expand to adjacent accounts based on proven capacity.

## 9. Human approval gates

Human approval is mandatory before:

- Bulk external messaging.
- Any external message presenting a partnership, customer, vendor, workforce quantity, or performance claim.
- Pricing or commercial commitments.
- Contracts or terms acceptance.
- Employment offers, contractor classification, pay changes, termination, or worker placement.
- Regulated work, licensed-trade representation, background-check use, safety-sensitive dispatch, or government procurement.
- Money movement.
- Production credential, permission, or destructive system changes.

## 10. Immediate message hierarchy

1. **Flex:** strategic distribution + Forge app/operations + carefully scoped workforce-support opportunity.
2. **Enterprise employers:** managed workforce/service-provider pilot + Forge app workflow.
3. **General contractors:** qualified specialty-trade and crew pipeline + intake/matching/reporting.
4. **Property/facilities operators:** recurring service-provider capacity + work-order-style routing and evidence.
5. **Logistics/distribution:** drivers, warehouse, mechanics, field service, and capacity reporting.
6. **Workers/providers:** real demand pipeline, transparent qualification, and no guarantee of work before a match is approved.

## 11. Operating principle

The 60 agents are the internal AI operations workforce. They research, organize, draft, analyze, and control the campaign. They are not interchangeable with human blue-collar workers. Human capacity is maintained in a separate verified supply inventory and is never promised before verification.
