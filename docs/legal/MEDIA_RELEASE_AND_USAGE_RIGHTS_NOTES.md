# Media Release And Usage Rights Notes

These are planning notes only. They are not final legal language and must be reviewed by an attorney before public launch or customer/provider use.

## Photo/Video Ownership

- Define whether the photographer/videographer retains copyright by default.
- Define whether the client receives a license to use final edited photos/videos.
- Define whether raw files, project files, or unedited footage are included, optional, or excluded.
- Define how ownership changes, if at all, for commercial work, contractor brand shoots, or paid ad creative.

## Client Usage Rights

- Specify allowed client use: personal sharing, website, social media, ads, print, listings, internal business use, and resale if applicable.
- Specify whether usage is personal, commercial, exclusive, non-exclusive, time-limited, territory-limited, or platform-limited.
- Confirm whether the client can edit, crop, filter, repost, or sublicense delivered media.

## Provider Portfolio Rights

- Define whether providers may use client images/videos in portfolios, websites, social channels, reels, ads, award submissions, or case studies.
- Require written client permission before portfolio use when the shoot includes minors, private homes, sensitive locations, private events, confidential business work, or unpublished products.
- Allow clients to opt out of provider portfolio use where appropriate.

## Minors / Family Shoot Release

- Require parent or legal guardian consent before using images/video of minors for portfolio, marketing, ads, or public case studies.
- Track whether family images are for private delivery only or may be used publicly.
- Avoid public use of minors' names, school details, home address, or sensitive personal context.

## Wedding / Event Release

- Clarify who can approve media use when many guests appear in event coverage.
- Define restrictions for private ceremonies, religious events, venues, performers, vendors, and guests who request privacy.
- Confirm venue rules for photography, videography, lighting, drones, and audio recording.

## Business Brand Shoot Release

- Confirm who owns or licenses brand, product, employee, job-site, vehicle, equipment, and customer testimonial imagery.
- Require business approval before using logos, uniforms, customer sites, client projects, or behind-the-scenes footage in public marketing.
- Address third-party trademarks, music, stock assets, and platform ad requirements.

## Drone / Insurance Notes

- Drone work should require appropriate qualifications, airspace/location review, property permission, weather/safety review, and insurance confirmation.
- Providers should disclose liability insurance status and any limitations before customer booking.
- Do not advertise drone availability as guaranteed until provider, location, and compliance checks pass.

## Attorney Review Required

Final customer terms, provider terms, media releases, copyright language, privacy language, and commercial usage rights must be reviewed by counsel before production use.
