Contractor FAQ

What joining Forge does—and does not—mean.

Do I need to be a general contractor?

No. Remodeling, commercial, specialty trade, project manager, crew, and individual-worker paths remain available where appropriate.

Does applying guarantee jobs?

No. Applying creates a reviewable profile and potential compatibility with opportunities; it does not guarantee work or response volume.

Will Forge call me verified?

Not from self-reported information. “Forge reviewed” requires actual review evidence, and expired or suspended states remain explicit.

Does Forge collect a fee or payment now?

No payment, escrow, payout, dispatch, or active Flex referral is enabled in this controlled-beta experience.
